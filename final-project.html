Gauge fields and the gravitational-wave spectrum from preheating

Final project — Gravitational Waves and AI-Assisted Research, UBA 2026

Scaffold — no runs yet

The question

Preheating after inflation sources a stochastic gravitational-wave background from the anisotropic stress of classical, inhomogeneous field configurations on the lattice. When the field content includes a gauge field, its own energy-momentum contributes to that stress, and its dynamics can differ sharply from scalar self-resonance. This project asks how much of the resulting GW spectrum's shape — peak frequency, peak amplitude, spectral slope either side of the peak — is attributable to the gauge field, holding the rest of the model fixed.

Approach

Using CosmoLattice's built-in Abelian-Higgs template: a scalar-only control run (gauge coupling e → 0) against a small grid of Abelian-Higgs runs at increasing e, everything else held fixed, comparing the extracted ΩGW(f) across the grid.
